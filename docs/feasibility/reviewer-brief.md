The first release covers **chapters 1 and 2 only**, serves learners **aged 15+**, including minors and adults, and explains questions through messenger bots. The reviewer adjudicates content and educational explanations; AI output cannot approve itself.

The owner must agree a rate, dates, and whether a second reviewer is needed for disputed keys. ## Review protocol

For each item record source/edition/page, stable question-family ID, stem/options, verified answer, explanation, scope/readability status, citations, reviewer identity/date, and disputes. Keep unreadable or disputed items explicit. A second model's agreement is not independent subject review.

Protect the 200-family holdout from generation/tuning agents and from the practice bank. Reviewer-approved labels should be held in restricted storage, not this public implementation tree. Once used for fixes, that set becomes regression evidence and a fresh independent release evaluation must be arranged.

Use synthetic or permissioned material for engineering spikes. For real student photos, arrange appropriate participation/data permissions before collection and remove unnecessary identifiers. Source possession and public availability alone do not establish reuse permission.
